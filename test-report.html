20Health score method

Each area's score is the share of its executed checks that pass, with WARN counted as half. The overall score is the weighted average of area scores, using the weights in the coverage table (Multi-tenancy 25, Security 15, Authentication 10, Authorization 10, Tickets 10, Integrations 8, and smaller weights for the rest). Because a CRITICAL security or isolation failure should dominate, an open CRITICAL defect caps the score at 60, and an open HIGH defect caps it at 75. FIXED bugs are excluded from the cap; MITIGATED bugs count as open. The weighted result here is 100; applied cap: 100. Blocked and not-implemented checks are excluded from the denominator, so they do not inflate the score, but they are listed.
